# Recommendation: Displaying Credits on the Pricing Page

## 1. Should You Mention Credits on the Pricing Page?
**Yes, but with professional framing.** 
Transparently displaying credit allowances builds strong trust and prevents user confusion when generating AI content:
- **Pro Card**: *"Includes **500 AI Credits / month** (~15 full topic packs or ~50 individual feature generations)"*
- **Max Card**: *"Includes **1,500 AI Credits / month** (~45 full topic packs or ~150 individual feature generations)"*
- **Elite Card**: *"**Unlimited Fair Use** (Zero credit caps for dedicated candidates)"*

---

## 2. Where Should Subscribers-Only Top-Ups Be Accessible?
Since top-ups are restricted **exclusively to active subscribers**, they should not clutter the public marketing pricing page. Instead, they belong in:
1. **The User Dashboard / Billing Settings**: A dedicated *"Add AI Credits"* card visible only when a user has an active Pro, Max, or Elite subscription.
2. **Low-Credit / Out-of-Credits Modal**: A polite pop-up that appears when a subscriber's credit balance hits 0, offering instant 1-click top-up packs ($5 for 800 credits or $10 for 1,600 credits) with a guaranteed 20% platform profit margin.
